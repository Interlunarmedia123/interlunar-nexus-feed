Meta & Google Ads

Meta removes ad-set-level placement exclusion controls

As of August 21, 2026, advertisers can no longer exclude individual placements at the ad-set level. If any live campaigns rely on placement exclusions to keep spend off underperforming surfaces, check them now - this is a workflow break, not a cosmetic change.

commonthreadco.com - Aug 21, 2026

Google Ads extends Limited Ad Serving policy to all accounts

Google is rolling out "Limited Ad Serving" - reduced impressions for ads/accounts judged more likely to produce a bad experience - across all Google Ads, phased in gradually through 2028. Worth knowing before a client account unexpectedly loses reach with no obvious policy strike.

support.google.com - Aug 2026

Google Ads adds Customer Lifecycle Optimization to conversion goals

Landed August 26, 2026, directly inside the conversion goals panel. Lets accounts optimize toward lifecycle stages instead of one-off conversions - relevant for any client with a retention or LTV play.

commonthreadco.com - Aug 26, 2026

Google Ads starts auto-labeling customer lists by type

From August 18, 2026, Google automatically assigns a customer type to every eligible conversion-based customer list that hasn't been manually labeled. Passive change, but it can shift how remarketing lists get treated - worth a spot check on client accounts.
